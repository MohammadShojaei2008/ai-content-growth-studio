# First paid client — reply, quote, payment, and delivery playbook

**Status:** Prepared internal operations material on 2026-11-22. No signed client, accepted quote, connected checkout or collected payment yet. Do not publish a prospect's private messages here. This file is for internal preparation, not a claim of existing business operations.

## Decision gate before accepting any paid work
- Check whether buyer is a real, consenting requester and whether the offered work is lawful, feasible and within the studio's actual scope.
- Ask explicitly whether **AI-assisted research, outlining and drafting** are permitted for their project; explain editorial review and claim-checking, and never claim entirely human drafting.
- Confirm topic, target audience/platform, duration/word count, sample references, brand voice, factual and legal constraints, delivery format, deadline/time zone, revisions, approved usage rights and agreed compensation currency.
- Make no guarantees about views, conversions, commercial performance, or copyright ownership beyond terms agreed with the actual rights holder. Script concepts must be original and should not reproduce third-party content.
- Do **not** collect or publish a client's sensitive information in GitHub. Use the private business inbox for briefs.
- Verify actual payment rails with their legal owner. **There is currently no connected business payment processor or functioning checkout.** The studio cannot honestly send an existing payment link. The user needs to decide/configure an eligible payment method if a buyer agrees; no fake link, no phantom invoice, no collecting account data in public.
- Do not start a substantive commissioned project or promise a delivery date until both parties have agreed on payment terms and the actual payment route works.
- Customer replies may be **drafted** for approval but not automatically sent under existing authorization, which covers new public paid-job application emails only.
