Mystery-box portrait challenge

Original five-minute YouTube creative strategy sample: title, thumbnail direction, cold open, and timed beat sheet.

Proposed title

I Let 3 Mystery Boxes Control My Celebrity Portrait — The Last One Ruined Everything

Thumbnail concept

Split-frame: near-finished portrait on the left, a hand holding an ominous red box on the right, with the host between them. Two-word overlay: “DESTROY IT?” The thumbnail asks a question the title does not answer.

Two-sentence hook

“I have five minutes to finish this portrait, but every mystery box makes the rules worse. One of these boxes says I have to destroy my own artwork—and I don’t get to choose when it appears.”

Five-minute pacing blueprint

TimeBeatVisual direction
0:00–0:12Cold openScissors near the portrait; smash cut to mystery boxes and visible countdown.
0:12–0:45SetupShow the reference image, materials, three boxes, and the fair rules.
0:45–1:35Round 1Dominant-color restriction and a visible before/after.
1:35–2:30Round 2Draw with the nondominant hand; focus on a defining feature.
2:30–3:25RecoveryA clever correction makes the portrait look surprisingly good.
3:25–4:20Final box“Destroy your artwork.” Tear a preplanned expendable copy, not the master.
4:20–5:00PayoffTurn torn pieces into a collage portrait; show a before/after.

Production safeguard

Use a removable paper copy for the tear, clear all music and reference imagery, and don't imply a real contest took place unless the rules were actually followed. Foreshadow the collage materials early for a satisfying ending.
