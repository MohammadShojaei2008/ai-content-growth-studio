Two Reels for fitness coaching

Two ready-to-film short-video concepts for coaches. These generic examples are not affiliated with or endorsed by any existing business.

01 — Strength AND endurance can fit in one plan

Hook (0–4 seconds): “You can want to get stronger and improve your endurance. You do not have to choose just one.”

Script (4–25 seconds): “Start with where you are. Pick one realistic strength goal and one endurance goal. Then build small sessions you can repeat. Progress is about consistency, not doing every workout at once.”

On-screen: Strength goal + endurance goal + a schedule you can sustain.

CTA: “Want coaching built around your goals? Send me a message.”

02 — Your first workout does not need to impress anyone

Hook (0–4 seconds): “Never trained before? That is a perfectly good place to start.”

Script (4–25 seconds): “You do not need to get in shape before asking for help. The right first session meets you at your current level. We focus on learning movements, building confidence, and making progress at a pace that fits your life.”

On-screen: Start at your level. Build from there.

CTA: “Curious about your first session? Let us talk.”

These concepts are creative samples and are not individualized health or exercise recommendations.
